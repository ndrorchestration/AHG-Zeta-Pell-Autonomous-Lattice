# Release and Evidence Policy

## Current posture

This repository is a research artifact concerning AHG, Zeta/Pell-related structures, and an autonomous lattice concept. Mathematical and control-system claims remain evidence-dependent.

## Versioning

Use `0.x.y` or dated research revisions while formal definitions, derivations, and experiments remain under development. Do not treat a research version as a certification level.

## Evidence rule

Project-local equations, generated sequences, simulations, and control proposals should be labelled according to what was actually established. Formal mathematical claims require derivation; numerical claims require reproducible computation; control-performance claims require measured experiments or validated simulation methodology.

Pass 2 and later evidence should update the repository's evidence checkpoint rather than silently upgrading descriptive language.
